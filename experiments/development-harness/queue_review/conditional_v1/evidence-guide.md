Prior local evidence: in two small queue tasks, solo repair and mandatory pre-review followed by a fresh
maker both passed all four behavioral checks. Pre-review took 1.842 times the submission time on the
defective implementation and 2.893 times on an already correct implementation; total input/output also
increased. Both methods left the correct implementation unchanged. These were one pair per scenario,
not a general result about reviews, and did not measure interrupted writes or concurrent writers.

Use this as local evidence, not a rule to avoid consultation. First use the public checks and your own
investigation/repair. If a specific material uncertainty remains, identify the conflicting or missing
evidence and ask a question that a separate reviewer can help resolve. If the requirements are adequately
supported by your observations, submit without adding a reviewer merely because one is available.
The meaning or usefulness of your uncertainty is not automatically graded; the final behavior is.
