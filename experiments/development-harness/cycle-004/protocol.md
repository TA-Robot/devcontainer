selected from the actual shared contract loader, where conflicting properties
are accepted with last-value-wins semantics. The task and its 22 executable
requirements are fixed before either developer runs. A correct independent
reference and last-wins, blanket-rejection, case-folding, value-leaking, and
validation-bypass mutants calibrate the observer. None are placed in the
candidate checkout or developer image.

The automated comparison executes both conditions, then evaluates every recorded
artifact against the identical 22 requirements and emits quality, time, usage,
budget completion and same-time comparisons. A speed ratio requires both final
artifacts to meet the fixed quality threshold within the observed budget.
This single pair is exploratory, not a general speed/quality claim.

The new task-specific threshold covers the shared loader and validate_file paths.

checks for integration, not a human override of the frozen comparison. Any
integration repair is outside developer timing and the original outcome remains.
